# Admissions resources

Sourced figures and practical notes on business school, Oxbridge and MIT admissions. Numbers come from the schools' own pages, which each article links.


## Business school

- [Executive MBA deadlines for 2027 entry](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-emba-deadlines-2027?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): EMBA application deadlines for 2027 entry, every round in one table: Wharton, Booth, Kellogg, Columbia, LBS, INSEAD, Oxford and more, from each school's site.
- [How to score in the 99th percentile on the GMAT](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-gmat-99th-percentile?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): On GMAC's August 2026 table a GMAT Focus 715 is the 98.6th percentile and 735 the first above 99%. Section cut-offs, and a plan from a two-time scorer.
- [GMAT or GRE for an MBA? What the class profiles show](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-gmat-or-gre-for-mba?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): GMAT Focus or GRE for an MBA in 2027: the test mix and median scores at HBS, Columbia, Kellogg and Wharton, how schools read each test, and how to choose.
- [LBS MBA interview questions, from someone who asks them](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-lbs-interview-questions?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): The questions London Business School MBA and EMBA interviews cover, how to answer each, and the mistakes that sink an interview. From a current LBS interviewer.
- [Is an MBA worth it? An answer for people in finance and tech](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-is-mba-worth-it?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Is an MBA worth it in 2026? The real cost, what graduates earn, the break-even maths, and when it pays off for bankers, engineers and quants.

## Oxford, Cambridge and MIT

- [Oxford and Cambridge acceptance rates by course](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-oxbridge-acceptance-rates?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Oxford maths made offers to 10.1% of applicants in 2025–26; Cambridge CS admitted 7.4%. Rates by course from both universities' own figures.
- [MIT's acceptance rate for international students](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-mit-international-acceptance-rate?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): MIT admitted 136 of 6,926 international applicants for the Class of 2029, 2.0%, against 5.4% for US applicants. Early action, deferrals, scores and aid.
- [Reading an Oxford MAT Question Like an Examiner](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/blog-oxford-interview?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Why students with perfect school marks score in the forties on MAT mocks, what the questions detect, and how the December interview differs from an exam.
- [MAT Preparation for Oxford: TMUA Replaces It for 2027 Entry](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/mat-preparation?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Oxford has replaced the MAT with the TMUA for 2027 entry. What changed, how the two tests differ, and how to prepare for both with worked questions.
- [STEP Preparation for Cambridge Maths](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/step-preparation?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): STEP 2 and STEP 3 preparation for Cambridge mathematics offer holders, taught personally by an Oxford computer science graduate and working HFT quant.
